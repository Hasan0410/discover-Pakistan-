Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Last updated: January 15, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Discover Pakistan collects personal details required to deliver safe, luxury travel services. This includes your name, passport/CNIC copy (required for high-altitude border permits and domestic flights), contact details, emergency contacts, payment information, and dietary or physical requirements.

2. Use of Information

Your information is solely utilized to arrange hotel reservations, private transport, local authority permits, and custom tour packages. We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal information to third-party marketing companies.

3. Data Security & Storage

All sensitive transactions and communication channels are encrypted with industry-standard TLS 1.3 encryption. Financial data is processed directly via PCI-DSS certified banking gateways.

4. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to review, update, or request the deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting our data protection officer at privacy@discoverpakistan.travel.
