Booking Terms & Traveler Agreement

Last updated: January 15, 2026

1. Booking & Confirmation Deposit

A non-refundable confirmation deposit of 30% is required at the time of bespoke itinerary approval. The remaining balance must be cleared at least 14 days prior to departure. For last-minute bookings within 14 days, full payment is required upon confirmation.

2. Weather & High-Altitude Itinerary Flexibility

High-altitude weather in Gilgit-Baltistan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa is dynamic. While every effort is made to maintain planned schedules, safety is our ultimate priority. We reserve the right to modify routes, passes (such as Babusar or Shandur Pass), or flights when official meteorological warnings are issued.

3. Rescheduling & Cancellation Policy

  • 30+ days before departure: 100% refund of refundable portion or free 12-month date change credit.
  • 15 to 29 days before departure: 50% refund of the total package value.
  • Under 14 days: Subject to partner hotel and transport cancellation fees.

4. Traveler Insurance & Health

All travelers undertaking high-altitude treks above 3,000 meters are required to possess valid comprehensive medical and evacuation insurance.
